One worn-in plugin for that lived-in tape sound — saturation, wow & flutter, lofi crunch, dubby tape echo, spring reverb and a tape stop you can play like an instrument.

    Six tape types from Chrome to 8 Track, aged from New to Dirty, with drive, saturation and harmonics.

  • Lofi

    Sample-rate and bit crushing, hiss, wow, flutter, wear dropouts and dust crackle.

  • Space

    Room, hall and plate reverbs, a dubby tape echo and a drippy spring.

  • Modulation

    Five LFO shapes on pitch, filter, amp or pan — from gentle warble to seasick.

  • Tape Stop

    Slow the whole mix to a halt and spin it back up — automatable in your DAW.
